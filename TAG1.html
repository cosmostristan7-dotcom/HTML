<hx> tags are used to define headings in HTML. The "x" in <hx> can be replaced with

a number from 1 to 6,

indicating the level of the heading. <h1> is the highest level heading, while <h6> is the lowest. Headings are important for structuring content and improving accessibility and SEO. For more information about what SEO means, click here.
