<p> <hx> TAGS
Function: they are to highlight a title, word, phrase or a whole paragraph. They are the [h1 - h6] tags. They have closing tags. They are known as Block Tags. 

They can devide our content into chapters (if that's what we need to do). Either by using the <h1> - <h6> tags or using the <hr color="red" width="50%"> lines format.
</p>

<p>"hr" TAGS
<h1 color:"red">Function:</h1> Make lines appear below or above a title, phrase or even a paragraph, they have no closing tag. Example:</p>

```html 
<HTML>
<head>
<title>Horizontal LINE rule</title>
</head>
<body>
Hello World
<hr color="green" width="50%">
<hr color="navy" width="70%">
</body>
</html> ```
